Is rhinoplasty painful?

Discomfort is managed under an anaesthesia and analgesia protocol prescribed by your surgeon. Tenderness and sensitivity to pressure are common for some weeks afterwards, and more extensive work on cartilage or bone generally means more discomfort. Your surgeon will set out what to expect in your case.*

How long do the results last?

Results are long-lasting, but ageing or injury can change the structure of the nose over time. No surgical result can be guaranteed to be permanent.*

Can I combine procedures in one trip?

Often yes, and combining is a genuine advantage of travelling for treatment. Whether it is appropriate is decided case by case at your in-person consultation, taking recovery and anaesthesia time into account.
